# Working Documentation Portfolio: Comprehensive Interior Design Projects in ArchiCAD (2025–2026)



## About the Specialist & Key Skills

Oleksii Kolodiazhnyi works as a **Junior Interior Designer, ArchiCAD Specialist, and Architectural Drafter**, available for project-based contracts (working remotely from Krakow, Poland).

**Key Focus Areas:**

* Professional development of comprehensive working drawing albums in **ArchiCAD (BIM)**.
* Creation of 3D documentation for structural nodes, sections, and complex joinery details.
* Detailed project procurement and drafting precise specifications for finishing materials, plumbing fixtures, and electrical fittings.
* Workflow optimization and acceleration using modern AI tools (Gemini, ChatGPT).

## Key Portfolio Case Studies

The portfolio features several large-scale residential projects completed in collaboration with the concept design studio *Dabood*. Every project is backed by a full package of technical construction documentation.

### Case 01. Apartment in a New Development (75 m², "Great" RC, Kyiv)

A full-scope residential interior project involving initial site measurement processing, layout iteration development, and the release of a complete working album.

* **Drawing Package:** As-built layout, demolition and construction plans, floor and ceiling plans, and wall finish layouts.
* Particular attention was paid to wet area wall elevations (kitchen and bathrooms) and electrical outlet specifications.

### Case 02. Studio Apartment Reconfiguration (33 m², Bahaliia St., Kharkiv)

A compact project requiring pinpoint accuracy in space planning and tile layout optimization.

* **Drawing Package:** 3D modeling of custom kitchen cabinetry, detailed tile layout patterns with waste calculations, and wall elevations featuring complex junction nodes.

### Case 03. Dual Independent Living Unit Project (84 m²)

A complex technical assignment requiring the division of space into two fully independent functional living units.

* **Drawing Package:** Drywall (GKL) partition plans, complex plumbing schematics with wall elevations for two bathrooms (Bathroom A and B), lighting group schematics, and detailed electrical component specifications (sockets, switches, and multi-gang frames accounting for IP20/IP44 ratings).
